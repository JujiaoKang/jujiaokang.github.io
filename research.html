Research

My research combines statistical methodology with biomedical discovery in genetics, neuroimaging, psychiatry, and multi-omics.

Theme 1

Statistical Genetics and Imaging Genetics

I study the genetic basis of brain structure, brain function, psychiatric disorders, and complex traits using GWAS summary statistics, neuroimaging data, and statistical modeling frameworks.

Theme 2

Causal Inference and Association Analysis

My methodological work includes Mendelian randomization, partial correlation analysis, genetic covariance modeling, and frameworks for distinguishing shared genetic signals from trait-specific effects.

Theme 3

Psychiatric Disorders and Brain Science

I apply statistical methods to depression, alcohol consumption, schizophrenia-related phenotypes, obesity-related brain aging, and cardiometabolic-psychiatric comorbidity.

Theme 4

Biomedical Big Data and Multi-omics

I work with large-scale genomic, proteomic, methylation, exome sequencing, neuroimaging, and behavioral datasets to identify interpretable biological pathways and biomarkers.

Methodological Interests

  • Bidirectional and exclusive Mendelian randomization
  • Genetic partial correlation and conditional genetic association analysis
  • Latent factor modeling for shared genetic structure
  • Multi-trait and multi-modal integration using summary statistics
  • Statistical learning for neuroimaging and biomedical data
