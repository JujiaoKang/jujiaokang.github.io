Biography

Jujiao Kang is a lecturer at the School of Mathematics and Statistics, Hunan Normal University. Her research lies at the interface of statistics, applied mathematics, biomedical engineering, genetic statistics, imaging genetics, and psychiatric research.

Her work combines statistical methodological development with large-scale biomedical applications, including neuroimaging, genetics, proteomics, exome sequencing, methylation data, and mental health phenotypes.

She is particularly interested in causal inference, genetic association analysis, partial correlation methods, multi-omics integration, and statistical modeling for brain-related complex traits.
